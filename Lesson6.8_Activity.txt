During testing, you discover a security vulnerability that could potentially expose customer data.

For the above scenario complete the following actions; 

1. List the relevant stakeholders who need to hear this information (developers, product owners etc.) 
2. List all the relevant risks you think apply 
3. Set a priority for the bug (use one of the techniques in the module) 
4. Draft an email to communicate your findings to the stakeholder list in action 1. 
    Remember that they may have different views and priorities so make sure you cover them all. 

1. development team (developers, other test roles, system architect, security team members), 
    business roles (product owner, business analyst, leadership)
2. risk to business confidence, reputation, trust, and private customer data including financial information
3. high impact, high likelyhood

    I have determined there is a security vulnerability within the feature we are working on.

    Due to our compliance requirements within an e commerce website, we must take steps to mitigate this risk before pushing the changes to production.
    Failure to mitigate this vulnerability risks damage to the business reputation and confidence in the company, fines for lack of security compliance, as well as damage to the customer when their financial information is exposed.

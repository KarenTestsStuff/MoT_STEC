Find something in your house and evaluate its quality in relation to you as the user of the item. 
Why was it chosen? Does it deliver value to your home? 
Does it have a different use for another member of the household?
Then think of something similar that is cheaper and one that is more expensive. 
Evaluate their quality against your item. Compare the differences between the three objects. 

Vacuum 

New one vs old one

As a user of the new vacuum, its quality is evaluated higher because of its relation to the old one. 
The previous iteration was very noisy and hard to manouver through the house and over thresholds.
The current model allows for easy one-handed pickup and change of suction level.
The current one is also cordless, so no worries about sucking up the cord while cleaning.

To my housemate, the current model is a major improvement because of the lesser noise. 
The old model caused her such stress that she had to use it with noise canceling earphones.
The current model is much quieter with less vibration, and better for her stress levels.
It doesn't really make a difference for me, but for her this quality aspect was important.

The old model was limited to use in the house only, where the new model is cordless and can be used on the car as well.

Attachments - easier to change and smaller to store
Bagless - easier to empty after each clean, less mess
Having to stop to change outlets - less steps means I'm more likely to do it more often

Activity: Reflect on how you can apply these techniques in your own projects. 
What questions could you ask, and what tools or strategies might 
help you identify risks effectively?

Question the requirements:
- make sure assumptions are noted and cleared
- all team members from technical and business team are on same page
- everyone working towards the same goals

Question from different user ability levels:
- elderly
- differently abled or assistive technology
- technologically savvy
Frustrations from one group might be easy for another, and vice versa

Questions from skill gaps in the team
- do we have the right expertise to understand and test for all scenarios?
- can we pull in an expert from another team?
- are there community resources available to us?
